Privacy

This site lists links to public Claude artifacts. Browsing needs no account.

Signing in with Google

Posting and voting require a Google sign-in. When you post or vote, Google confirms your sign-in to our server. We do not receive your password and do not store your email address.

  • Posts: the link, title, description, tags and screenshot you submit are published together with the first name of your Google account.
  • Votes: we store a salted hash of your Google account id so each account can vote once. The hash cannot be turned back into your account.

Posts and vote hashes are stored in a public GitHub repository and are visible to everyone.

Service providers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, the server runs on Cloudflare Workers, sign-in uses Google Identity Services, and the stylesheet is loaded from jsDelivr. These providers process technical data such as your IP address, and Google sets cookies for the sign-in. The site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising.

Removal and contact

To have a post or your votes removed, open an issue in the GitHub repository.
